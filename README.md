# 🚲 Bicycle Safety Analysis – Predictive Modeling for Safer Roads

### 👋 About This Project
This project is an in-depth analysis aimed at improving cyclist safety across Pennsylvania. I analyzed historical bicycle accident data to identify risk factors and developed predictive models to help stakeholders implement targeted safety measures.

### 🎯 What I Aimed to Achieve
- Pinpoint key risk factors contributing to bicycle fatalities and severe injuries.
- Build predictive models to forecast and prevent high-risk incidents.

### 🛠️ How I Did It (Skills & Tools Used)
- **Machine Learning & Predictive Analytics** *(Logistic Regression, Random Forest, Scikit-learn, Python)*
- **Data Exploration & Visualization** *(Pandas, NumPy, Tableau, Matplotlib, Seaborn)*
- **Geospatial Analysis & Mapping** *(Cluster Mapping, GeoPandas)*
- **Insightful Data Storytelling & Presentation**

### 📈 What I Found
- Urban areas accounted for 94% of cyclist fatalities.
- The presence of stop signs significantly decreased severe incidents by 84%.
- My predictive models achieved high accuracy (~89%), enhancing decision-making capabilities for safety officials.

### 🌟 Recommendations Delivered
- Increase installation of stop signs and signals at urban mid-blocks and intersections.
- Implement targeted safety campaigns during peak accident hours (4-6 pm).

### 🙋‍♂️ My Role in This Project
- Designed and built predictive models (Logistic Regression, Random Forest).
- Performed detailed exploratory data analyses to extract actionable insights.
- Presented key findings and recommendations effectively to stakeholders.
